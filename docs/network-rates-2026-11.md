# Network rates (Florida averages), November 2026

Published on report "Fix it" cards and in the insurance packet as "network rate"
for services Palma sources from its vendor network. Each figure is the average of
10 distinct, publicly posted Florida prices, each confirmed on the source page on
2026-11-03. Single prices count as both low and high. Re-check yearly.
